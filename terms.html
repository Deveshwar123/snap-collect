Snap Match: Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short: Snap Match is a game, and it is also part of a personal research project. When you play, your snap recordings are used to train software that recognizes finger snaps and tries to tell people apart by their snap. Your nickname and points appear on the leaderboard only if you choose. You can ask for your recordings to be deleted at any time.

1. Who runs this

Snap Match is run by an individual as a personal, non-commercial project (the "organizer"), not by a company. You can contact the organizer by direct message on Instagram, through the account that shared this link.

2. What is collected when you play

  • Your snap recordings: short audio clips (about 3 seconds each) recorded through your device's microphone while you play, or a file you choose to upload. Recordings can also pick up background sound, including voices nearby.
  • Your name or nickname, as you type it.
  • Game results: match scores, points, rounds played and your leaderboard choice.
  • Basic technical details: your browser and device type, language setting, time zone and the time of each round, sent automatically by your browser.

Snap Match does not use your camera, location, contacts, email address or social media account, and it does not set advertising or tracking cookies.

3. How your recordings are used

  • To train and test software that detects finger snaps, and software that tries to recognize whether two snaps came from the same person.
  • To measure how accurate that software is.
  • To describe the project's results in summaries or write-ups, which never include your recordings, your name, or anything that identifies you.

Your recordings are not sold, not published, and not shared with anyone for their own use. Trained software does not contain your recordings. Inside the project's data your recordings are labeled with a random ID, not your name.

4. The game and the leaderboard

  • Match scores and points are worked out by the game for fun. They are not a measurement of you, and there are no prizes.
  • If you tick "Show my nickname on the leaderboard", your nickname and total points are visible to everyone who plays. Untick it and nothing about you is shown.
  • Please don't use your full name, or anything offensive or impersonating someone else, as a nickname. The organizer may remove nicknames or scores that break these terms, or that look like cheating.

5. Where your data is stored and for how long

Recordings and game results are stored privately in the organizer's Google account (Google Drive and Google Sheets). They are kept for as long as the project is active, and for no more than 3 years after your last game. After that they are deleted.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Playing is voluntary. You can stop at any time; rounds you haven't finished are not saved.
  • You can ask for all of your recordings and game results to be deleted at any time. Send a direct message on Instagram with the nickname you used, and roughly when you played. They will be deleted within 30 days.
  • Software that has already been trained can't "un-learn" a recording, but deleted recordings are never used again.
  • You can ask what data is held about you, and the organizer will tell you.

7. Age

You must be at least 13 to play. If you are under 18, you need a parent or guardian's permission. If a parent or guardian asks for a child's recordings to be deleted, they will be.

8. What you agree to

  • You will only record or upload your own snaps, and you won't upload recordings of other people or of copyrighted material.
  • You give the organizer permission to store and use your recordings and game results as described in these terms, for this project only.

9. No warranty

Snap Match is provided as is, without any guarantee that it works on your device or that scores are accurate. Any data you submit may be lost; please don't rely on the game to keep anything.

10. Changes

If these terms change, the date at the top will change. Changes apply to games played after that date. Your earlier recordings will only be used under the new terms if the changes don't reduce your rights; otherwise they will be deleted on request.
